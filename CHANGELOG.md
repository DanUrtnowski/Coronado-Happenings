changelog. Nothing is listed until it is genuinely reached.

## 2026-11-03 — Project record established

- Public showcase repository created.
- Publication scope defined: a hyperlocal community publication for Coronado.
- Endless-scrolling reading model recorded as a design constraint, including the
  requirements it creates for position retention and honest URLs.
- Ingestion principles defined: attributed sources, labelled machine collection,
  human editorial gate before publication.
- Advertising documented as a **prospective** system, separated from editorial
  content, with no behavioural targeting and no reader profiles.

  import has been claimed or performed.
